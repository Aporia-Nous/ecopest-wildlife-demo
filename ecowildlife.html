About this recording This is a screen recording of the system built for this business — the actual call flow, showing how a call is routed and what happens at each step. It is the working build, not a concept. Questions the system will not answer on its own — anything about pricing, wildlife law, or how the animals are handled — are routed to the owner instead of being guessed at.
